## 🚀 What Is little-chits?

little-chits is a simulation that runs on your computer and shows a small world of AI creatures. These creatures are not scripted actors following a fixed story. They are tiny agents with memories, goals, and access to an LLM. That means they can react to what they find, talk with each other, build on ideas, and teach what they know.

The experience is designed for watching. You open the app, see the world, and follow the creatures as they explore. Some may discover a new tool. Some may build a small structure. Some may learn a skill from another creature and then improve it. Over time, knowledge spreads through the civilisation. You can leave it running like a virtual terrarium and check back to see what changed.

Because little-chits works with any LLM in the creatures’ minds, the world can feel curious, practical, creative, or chaotic depending on the mind you choose. You do not need to understand how LLMs work to enjoy it. The app handles the technical parts. Your job is to watch, click around, and follow the stories that emerge.

## 🧠 How the Little Chits Think

Each little chit is an AI agent with an LLM in its mind. In simple terms, the LLM is the part that helps the creature make sense of the world and decide what to do next. The creature can observe its surroundings, remember recent events, and choose an action. It can also talk with other creatures and share knowledge.

Because the LLM is doing the thinking, the creatures are not limited to a small list of canned responses. They can combine ideas, respond to changes, and develop new behaviours. One creature might figure out a better way to build. Another might learn that method and teach it to a younger creature. Over time, the civilisation can develop its own culture of knowledge.

little-chits is designed around the idea that any LLM can be used. You can choose a local LLM if you want the simulation to run on your own computer. You can choose a hosted LLM if you prefer a service. The app is meant to make that choice understandable, even for users who have never worked with AI models before.
